About Me

Entry-level IT professional who enjoys fixing problems and building reliable systems.

I’m a recent BS Information Technology graduate from STI College Sta. Mesa. During my back-end internship at WeSupport Incorporated, I built PHP and MySQL features for an inventory and email management system. I also diagnosed bugs and database issues and helped test the system before release. For my capstone, I developed a POS and inventory system for a local pastry business that replaced manual record-keeping and reduced errors.

Alongside development, I have a solid base in IT support: desktop and hardware troubleshooting, software installation, and networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP). I like work where I can find the cause of a problem, fix it, and help people get back to what they were doing. I’m looking for an entry-level role in IT support, application support, or software development where I can keep learning and contribute from day one.
